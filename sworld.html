Science is your weapon.

Sworld is an action roguelike where the science is the combat. Coat your blade in alkali to neutralise acid slimes, dissolve metal shells and fight room by room to restore the lost laws of nature.

The game

Fast fights. Real science.

Sworld plays like a proper action game: dodge, combo, pick upgrades, beat the boss. You win by understanding the science, not by answering quiz pop-ups. If a concept would only work as a multiple-choice question, it isn’t in the game.

Real gameplay

The Equilibrium, the set of laws that holds Sworld together, has been shattered by Entropy, a rogue scientist-spirit. Every realm has lost one law and its Guardian has been corrupted. You are an apprentice of the last Lab. Master each law in combat and it comes back to you as a permanent power.

  1. Gear up in the LabYour hub. Pick a hero and loadout, read the Codex, spend Insight on permanent upgrades.
  2. Fight through a runEight to twelve rooms per realm: combat rooms, puzzle rooms, an elite and a boss.
  3. Choose a FormulaClear a room, then take one of three upgrades. Each is a piece of real science that changes how you fight.
  4. Fall, learn, go againWin or lose, you keep your Insight and every Codex entry. Each run starts a little stronger.
The realms

Three realms. Three lost laws.

Each realm is one science and every enemy in it follows that science’s rules. Learn the rule and the fight gets easier.

Why it works

Not a worksheet with a sword.

The science decides who wins the fight. That’s why it sticks.

Colour means something

Read the indicator.

Every slime carries a pH meter in universal-indicator colours. Red and orange are acids, green is neutral, blue and purple are alkalis. Overshoot pH 7 and the slime turns alkaline and dangerous again.

Real gameplay
Formulas

Upgrades are science.

Powdered Reagent dissolves shells twice as fast, because a powder has more surface area. Each card explains the science in one line.

Codex
The Codex

Revision you earn.

Each concept you master unlocks a short illustrated entry with the key fact or equation. Use it before a test, not just before a boss.

Show · Try · Apply · Twist

See it, try it, use it, then the twist.

A concept appears visually, you try it safely, you use it in a real fight, and then the game flips it: an enemy that is already neutral, a shell your acid can’t touch.

Mastery tracking

The Lab remembers.

Every encounter is tagged with its concept. Sworld keeps track of the choices you get right and wrong and shows your weak topics back in the Lab.

No dead ends

Mistakes hurt. They don’t stop you.

A wrong choice costs health or time, never your progress. Get stuck on the same idea and a hint appears.

Real gameplay
Three heroes

Pick who you fight as.

Three lab-explorer heroes with their own look, all wielding an energy blade whose colour shows your coating. Plays with keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.
