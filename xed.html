    Level 3 · Your moves

    Level 4 · Mini-game

    Make-10 Blitz

    A taste of Xed’s number bonds. Thirty seconds on the clock. How high can you go?

    1. Find the missing number that makes the total.
    2. Tap it, or press keys 1 to 4.
    3. Answer in a row to build a combo. Combos multiply your points.
    4. Hit a 5-combo and the totals jump from 10 to 20 to 100.
