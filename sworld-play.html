Play Sworld in your browser

Three worlds of slimes, spitters and metal-shelled monsters where the chemistry is the combat. The first two rooms are free for everyone; sign in inside the game with your MindsPlay class code to play all twelve rooms and face the three guardians. Best on a laptop or desktop with a keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad.

Science is your weapon.

Sound on! Read each monster’s colour: red and orange are acids, purple is alkali. Coat your blade with the opposite and bring them to green.

  • AttackLeft click or J
  • DodgeSpace or right click
  • Switch acid / alkaliQ or mouse wheel
