Back to numbers

Register a school’s class, print a take-home slip for every pupil, and see who has linked a parent. Pupils can play straight away with the PIN on their slip. When a parent scans the slip, the child moves into the parent’s account, where they see progress and pay.

Add pupils to a class

How parents pay

  • Free week, then each family pays. Every new pupil has 7 days free. The slip invites the parent to link the child. After that the parent pays like any other family, and partner schools earn their 5%.
  • School pilot. Set “Pilot until” for the school on the numbers page. Every pupil there plays free until then, and parents who linked get a WhatsApp reminder 3 days before it ends.
  • The school pays for the class. Open the class and set “Paid by school until”. Parents of that class see nothing to pay until that date.
