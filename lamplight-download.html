Download Lamplight

Lamplight: Bible Adventures, version 1.0, with fifteen stories from Noah’s Ark to The Lost Coin and about 2 to 3 hours of play. Lumi, a little lamp, reads every line aloud. Anyone can play the first two stories; sign in inside the game with your MindsPlay class code for all fifteen. Progress is saved on the computer, and after signing in once it plays without internet.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 98 MB.

Download for Windows
  1. Open the downloaded Lamplight-Windows.zip and choose Extract all.
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click Lamplight.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. About 106 MB.

Download for Mac
  1. Open Lamplight-Mac.zip and drag Lamplight into Applications.
  2. Double-click Lamplight. The first time, your Mac says it can’t check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Lamplight, then confirm with your password.
  4. On macOS 14 or older, you can instead right-click Lamplight and choose Open, then Open again.

How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys and look around with the mouse. Press E or click to talk and use things, and Space to jump. Answer Lumi’s questions with 1, 2 or 3. P pauses.
