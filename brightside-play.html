Play Brightside in your browser

Three friends explore a riverside town and fix real community problems the way engineers do: a footbridge, solar lights for the basketball court and rain harvesting for the garden. Three missions and a block-party finale, about 30 to 45 minutes. One mission of your choice is free for everyone; sign in inside the game with your MindsPlay class code for all three. Best on a laptop or desktop with a keyboard and mouse.

Fix your town like an engineer.

Sound on! Talk to neighbours, look for clues, then design your fix on the blueprint table.

  • LookMouse or arrow keys
  • Talk / inspectE or Space
