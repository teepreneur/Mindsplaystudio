Download GranED Prix

A 3D kart racer where knowledge is speed. Maths questions float over the track and you answer by steering into the right lane. This early version has the Numbera Canyon Cup, Time Trial and 1- or 2-player split screen, with questions for grades 1 to 8. It plays without internet.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 98 MB.

Download for Windows
  1. Open the downloaded GranEDPrix-Windows.zip and choose Extract all.
  2. Open the GranED Prix folder and double-click GranEDPrix.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

macOS 12 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. About 106 MB.

Download for Mac
  1. Open GranEDPrix-Mac.zip and drag GranED Prix into Applications.
  2. Double-click GranED Prix. The first time, your Mac says it can’t check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to GranED Prix, then confirm with your password.
  4. On macOS 14 or older, you can instead right-click GranED Prix and choose Open, then Open again.

How to play

Drive with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Space or Shift to drift, press E for items and Q for Double-or-Nothing. Answer by steering into the right lane. Two players can share one keyboard, and gamepads work too.
