Windows · Mac

Download Xed

Free to download. To play, your child signs in with the class code and picture PIN you get when you sign them up (the first week is free). After signing in once, Xed plays without internet.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 200 MB.

Download for Windows
  1. Open the downloaded XedQuest-Windows.zip and choose Extract all.
  2. Open the folder and double-click XedQuest.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

macOS 12 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. About 220 MB.

Download for Mac
  1. Open XedQuest-Mac.zip and drag XedQuest into Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click (or Control-click) XedQuest and choose Open, then Open again.

Then sign in

On the title screen, press Sign in with class code, type the class code (like XED-K7P4), tap your child’s name, then their 4 secret pictures. Brothers and sisters can each sign in on the same computer.
