Lap 1 · About the game

Knowledge is speed.

Pick a racer and hit the Numbera Canyon Cup. Three times a lap you reach an Answer Gate with a maths question over it and three answer lanes underneath. Steer into the right one for a boost. Pick the wrong one and you slow down for a moment, then keep racing.

Lap 2 · The levels

Eight grades. One race.

Every racer gets questions at their own grade, from adding to 20 all the way up to equations, squares and roots. Inside each grade the game watches how you do: three right and the questions get harder, two wrong and they get easier. Tap a grade to see what it covers.

A gate at this grade
The Numbera Canyon Cup

Digit Dunes and Mesa Multiplier

Two races through a desert canyon full of sandstone mesas, giant number monuments, cactus and hot-air balloons. Score points in each race to win the cup, or take on Time Trial and beat your best lap.

Lap 4 · What makes it different

The maths is the steering wheel.

There’s no quiz screen and no pause to answer. You think while you drive.

Two players, two grades

Race a friend at your own level.

Two players share one screen, and each gets questions at their own grade. A 7-year-old and a 12-year-old can race fairly.

Learns as you race

Always the right challenge.

Three right in a row and the questions step up. Two wrong and they ease off. Progress is saved for each player.

Mistakes come back

Missed it? You’ll see it again.

A question you got wrong comes back a few gates later, asked a different way, so it sticks. Wrong answers are built from real, common mistakes.

Did you know?

A Brain Report after every race.

The results screen shows the places, the cup points and a short “Did you know?” with the facts you missed, so you go back in knowing more.

Idea Orbs

Items with brains.

Grab Idea Orbs for the Eureka Bulb, Prism Shield, Magnet of Attraction, Gravity Well or Eraser Cloud. Feeling brave? Go Double-or-Nothing: answer a harder question to super-size your item.

Real kart racing

Drift, spark, boost.

Hold a drift to charge blue, orange and then purple sparks, and let go to boost. Last place gets Big Brain, which answers the next three gates for you.

Lap 5 · Mini-game

Gate Dash

A taste of GranED Prix in 45 seconds. Steer into the right answer at every gate and see how far you can drive.

  1. Read the question at the top.
  2. Steer with the arrow keys, A and D, keys 1 to 3, or tap a lane.
  3. Right lane: Spark Boost. Wrong lane: you slow down.
  4. Three right in a row makes the questions harder. Two wrong makes them easier.
