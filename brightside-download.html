Download Brightside

Three friends explore a riverside town and fix real community problems the way engineers do: a footbridge, solar lights for the basketball court and rain harvesting for the garden. Three missions and a block-party finale, about 30 to 45 minutes. It plays without internet.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 0 MB.

Download for Windows
  1. Open the downloaded Brightside-Windows.zip and choose Extract all.
  2. Open the Brightside folder and double-click Brightside.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. About 0 MB.

Download for Mac
  1. Open Brightside-Mac.zip and drag Brightside into Applications.
  2. Double-click Brightside. The first time, your Mac says it can’t check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Brightside, then confirm with your password.
  4. On macOS 14 or older, you can instead right-click Brightside and choose Open, then Open again.

How to play

Move with WASD and hold Shift to run. Look with the mouse or arrow keys and scroll to zoom. Press E or Space to talk and inspect, Tab to switch friend, and Esc for the pause menu. A gamepad works too.
