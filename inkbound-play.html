Play Inkbound in your browser

A storybook adventure set inside an open book. You are the Scribe: catch golden words and write them onto the world to repair what the Eraser rubbed out. Two chapters, on adjectives and verb tenses, about 15 to 20 minutes. Chapter One is free for everyone; sign in inside the game with your MindsPlay class code for the whole book. Best on a laptop or desktop with a keyboard and mouse.

Write the world back.

Walk up to a glowing prompt, press E to write, then choose the word that fixes the page.

  • WalkWASD or arrow keys
  • LookHold a mouse button and drag
  • JumpSpace
  • WriteE or Enter
  • Choose a word1, 2, 3 or click
