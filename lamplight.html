About the game

Lamplight: Bible Adventures

Children explore the Story Lands, a bright open world of hills, rivers, tents and shores. Lamp-lit paths lead to each story. There they gather wood for Noah, find five smooth stones for David, carry a boy’s lunch to Jesus and much more, so the story is something they do, not only something they hear.

Lumi, a little clay lamp, travels with them. Every line is read aloud, so children who are still learning to read can play on their own. Each story ends with a sticker to collect and a memory verse from the World English Bible.

The stories

Fifteen stories to step into.

Each story is told closely from the Bible in simple words, and Lumi asks two questions at the end. Choose one of the first six to see it in the game.

Chapter 1 · Genesis 6 to 9

Noah’s Ark

Find six logs in the forest so Noah can finish the ark, then lead the animals in two by two. Ride out forty days of rain, see the dove return with an olive leaf and look up at the rainbow of God’s promise.

★Rainbow sticker
“I set my rainbow in the cloud, and it will be a sign of a covenant between me and the earth.”Genesis 9:13
Chapter 1 · 1 Samuel 17

David and Goliath

Go with David to King Saul, watch him try on armour that is far too heavy, and find five smooth stones in the brook. Then stand with David in the valley and help him swing his sling.

★Brave Heart sticker
“The battle is the LORD’s.”1 Samuel 17:47
Chapter 1 · John 6

Five Loaves and Two Fish

Help Andrew find the boy with five barley loaves and two small fish, and carry his basket to Jesus. Share the food with more than five thousand people, then gather up twelve baskets of leftovers.

★Sharing sticker
“I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to me will not be hungry.”John 6:35
Chapter 2 · Luke 15

The Lost Sheep

Help a shepherd count his hundred sheep as evening falls. When one little lamb is missing, follow the tufts of her wool, find her and carry her safely home.

★Found sticker
“Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep which was lost!”Luke 15:6
Chapter 2 · Daniel 6

Daniel and the Lions

Pray with Daniel by his window, as he does every day. Wait through the long night with King Darius, then hurry to the den at sunrise and say hello to the gentle lions.

★Faithful sticker
“My God has sent his angel, and has shut the lions’ mouths, and they have not hurt me.”Daniel 6:22
Chapter 2 · Mark 4

Jesus Calms the Storm

Help Peter carry four fishing baskets to the boat and set sail with Jesus. When a great storm blows up, hear Jesus speak to the wind and the waves.

★Peace sticker
“Peace! Be still!” The wind ceased, and there was a great calm.Mark 4:39

And nine more in version 1.0: Baby Moses, Joshua and Jericho, Samuel Hears God’s Call, Ruth and Naomi, The First Christmas, The Wise Builder, The Good Samaritan, Zacchaeus and The Lost Coin.

What makes it different

Not a cartoon to watch. A story to walk into.

Real gameplay
Do the story

Help, don’t just watch.

Carry, count, lead and share. Each task comes straight from the story, so children remember what happened because they helped it happen.

Real gameplay
Read aloud

Every line, out loud.

Lumi and every character speak their lines, so six-year-olds who are still learning to read can play on their own.

Real gameplay
Faithful to the Bible

Told closely, in simple words.

We follow the Bible text and quote memory verses from the World English Bible. Lamplight is not tied to any one denomination, and we welcome guidance from teachers, pastors and Sunday school leaders.

Stickers and verses

Something to keep.

Every story ends with a sticker and a memory verse, read aloud. Sparkling lights and “Did you know?” scrolls wait along the paths for curious explorers.

Hymn-tune music

Songs they may already know.

Each place has its own hymn tune, from Amazing Grace and Jesus Loves Me to When the Saints Go Marching In.

Made for our children

Faces like theirs.

The people of the stories are Black, in many brown skin tones, so children see themselves in the story. No ads, no in-game purchases and no chat with strangers.

Two by Two

In the game, children help Noah lead the animals into the ark. Here is a little taste. No clock, no rush.

  1. Turn over two cards.
  2. If the animals match, they walk into the ark together.
  3. Fill the ark to earn the Rainbow sticker and a memory verse.

Tap the cards, or use Tab and Enter on a keyboard.
