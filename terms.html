Terms and privacy

MindsPlay Studios, Accra, Ghana · Last updated 20 October 2026

In short

  • A parent or guardian signs up and adds their children. Children never give us their own details.
  • We keep the least we need: your name, email and phone, and each child’s first name, school, class and learning progress.
  • We use it to run Xed, show you your child’s progress, remind you about renewals, and improve our games. We never show ads and never sell data.
  • You can remove a child (and all their progress) from your account at any time, or ask us to delete your whole account.

Your account

You must be 18 or older and the parent or guardian of each child you add. Keep your password private. One account can have up to 6 children.

Free week, plans and payments

Every new account gets 7 days free. After that, access is paid by the month, term (3 months) or year, with a lower price for each extra child. Payments are processed by Paystack; we never see your full card or Mobile Money PIN. Mobile Money payments cover the period you choose. Card payments can renew automatically; you can switch this off in your account at any time, and it takes effect from the next period. If something goes wrong with a payment, email us within 14 days and we’ll put it right.

Schools and class codes

Children in the same school class share a class code. Today it lets a child sign in with their name and picture PIN. In future it may let classmates play together and send pre-set messages inside the game; we will tell you before this starts. Schools in a pilot or partnership with us may see their pupils’ progress in Xed.

What we collect and why

  • Parent: name, email, phone, how you heard about us, payments. To run your account, contact you and keep records.
  • Child: first name, school, class, picture PIN (stored scrambled), game progress, answers to questions, time played, and the device type. To run the game, save progress across computers and show you reports.
  • Website visitors: anonymous counts of visits to the demo, sign-up and download pages. No advertising trackers.

Data is stored with Cloudflare. We follow Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). You may ask to see, correct or delete your data: email hello@mindsplaystudiogh.com.

Fair use

Xed is for your family’s personal learning. Please don’t share sign-ins outside your family, copy the game or try to break it. We may suspend accounts that do.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms; we’ll email you about important changes. Questions: hello@mindsplaystudiogh.com.
